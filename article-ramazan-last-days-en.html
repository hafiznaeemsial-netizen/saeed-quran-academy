Home / English Blog / Final Days of Ramadan

Days are few, but the door is not yet closed.

Share on Facebook Share on WhatsApp

Today is the 28th day of Ramadan. As the sun begins to set, the echoes of this glorious month start to fade. It is a moment naturally filled with sadness and reflection on whether we made the most of these divine blessings. However, amidst this bittersweet departure, a voice of mercy and urgency remains constant: Days are few, but the door is not yet closed.

Allah (SWT) is constantly calling His servants. Every third part of the night, He descends to the lowest heaven and asks: "Who is calling upon Me, that I may answer him? Who is asking Me, that I may give him? Who is seeking My forgiveness, that I may forgive him?" During these final nights, that divine call becomes even more profound.

1. The Significance of Final Moments: Hope Over Despair

The last ten days are the subset of "freedom from the Fire." At this very moment, the Shaytan is at his most desperate because he watches as sinners turn into saints through a single night of sincere tears. If you feel you have been slow, if you haven’t felt the spiritual high you expected, do not give up now.

These last few days and nights have the potential to rewrite the script of your entire life. Allah (SWT) does not weigh the quantity of your hours but the quality of your sincerity. A single tear of true repentance can evaporate a mountain of negligence. Stand before Him tonight and say: "O My Lord, I have nothing to present, but You are the All-Merciful, please accept me."

2. Reflection from the Heart: Moving Forward

As we discussed in our previous article "When Ramadan Departs", many hearts bow with the weight of regret as the month leaves. But regret is only useful if it pushes you towards action. If your regret leads to despair, it is from the whispering of Shaytan.

Seize the blessings of the 28th Iftar and the coming Suhoor. Every minute spent in Dhikr, Istighfar, and Du'a is a seed planted for eternity. This is the time when angels are distributing rewards under the command of the Al-Mighty. Do not be among the heedless.

3. Establishing a Spiritual Foundation for Children

Ramadan teaches us that humans are capable of profound transformation. This discipline shouldn't be for us alone. Ensuring our children are part of this spiritual ecosystem is an obligation. Enrolling them in a Quranic academy isn’t just about a school course—it’s about nurturing their souls.

A Critical Message for Parents!

The message of Ramadan is to connect with the Word of the Al-Mighty. Enroll your kids in Saeed Quran Academy so they can learn Tajweed, Quranic Arabic, and Islamic Ethics. Your decision today will be their shield in this world and the hereafter.

4. Laylat al-Qadr: The Grand Opportunity Remains

While some of the odd nights have passed, the 29th night is still ahead. Allah has described a single night as better than a thousand months. One minute of worship on this night could be worth years of devotion. It is the night where destinies are decreed. Fortunate is the one who finds this night in a state of worship.

5. Our Mission: Spreading the Light Worldwide

Saeed Quran Academy isn't just a place to recite words; it is a movement to plant the love of the Al-Mighty in the heart. We provide an environment where you can connect with your Deen from anywhere in the world—UK, USA, Australia, or Pakistan.

Visit our English Blog where we continuously upload guides to help you navigate your spiritual journey.

Conclusion

The door is open, the time for repentance is now, and Allah is waiting for His servant to run back to him. Days are few, but the door is not yet closed. Share this message with those you love, become a part of this goodness, and expect only the best from Allah. May He give us the ability to cherish these final moments of Ramadan. Ameen.
