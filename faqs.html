Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our classes, schedules, and fee payment.

Q: Do you offer trial classes?

A: Yes, we offer 3 days of free trial classes to all new students so you can evaluate our teaching style.

Q: Are there female teachers available?

A: Yes, we have qualified and experienced female teachers for sisters and young girls.

Q: How can I pay the fee?

A: You can pay via Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Western Union. Local students in Pakistan can pay via EasyPaisa or JazzCash.
