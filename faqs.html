Have Questions? We Have Answers

What is the duration of each class?

Standard classes are 30 minutes long, but we can adjust this according to the student's needs and concentration level.

Do you provide female teachers for sisters and kids?

Yes, we have a dedicated team of highly qualified female teachers available for our sisters and young children.

How can I pay the fee?

We accept payments through various secure methods including PayPal, Bank Transfer, and Western Union, depending on your location.

Is there any registration fee?

No, there is absolutely no registration fee. You only pay for the classes after your 3-day free trial.

What equipment do I need for online classes?

All you need is a smartphone, tablet, or laptop with a stable internet connection and a pair of headphones for better sound quality.
