Our Story & Mission

Learn how Saeed Quran Academy is helping thousands of students connect with the Divine words through modern technology.

Empowering the Muslim Ummah

Saeed Quran Academy was founded with the vision to make high-quality Islamic education accessible to everyone, regardless of their geographical location. We understand the challenges of living in non-Muslim countries where finding expert Quran tutors can be difficult.

Our teachers are not just experts in Tajweed and Hifz, but also trained in using digital tools to provide an engaging learning experience for children and adults alike.
