Terms & Conditions

1. Enrollment & Trial

Students are entitled to a 3-day free trial period. Regular classes will commence only after the student/parent is satisfied and the first month's fee is settled.

2. Attendance & Punctuality

Students must be online at least 2 minutes before the class starts. In case of an absence, please inform the administration at least 6 hours in advance to reschedule.

3. Fee Policy

Fees are collected in advance at the start of each month. Fees are non-refundable once the regular classes have started.

4. Code of Conduct

Both teachers and students are expected to maintain a respectful and Islamic decorum during the classes.
