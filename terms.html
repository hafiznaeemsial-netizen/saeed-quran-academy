Terms and Conditions

Welcome to Saeed Quran Academy. By enrolling in our courses, you agree to the following terms and conditions:

1. Enrollment and Classes

We provide 1-on-1 online classes. The duration of each session is typically 30 minutes unless agreed otherwise. Students are expected to be on time for their scheduled classes.

2. Fee Payment

Fees must be paid in advance at the start of each month. We offer various payment methods including Bank Transfer, PayPal, and Western Union. Failure to pay on time may result in suspension of classes.

3. Cancellations and Rescheduling

If a student cannot attend a class, they must inform the teacher at least 6 hours in advance. Makeup classes will be provided for informed absences at the teacher's convenience.

4. Code of Conduct

Students and teachers must maintain a respectful and professional environment. Any form of harassment or inappropriate behavior will result in immediate termination of the enrollment.
