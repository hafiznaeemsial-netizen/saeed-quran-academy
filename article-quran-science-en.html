The Holy Quran and Modern Science: 1400 Years of Miraculous Revelations

Scientific Miracles Islamic Blog
Share on Facebook Share on WhatsApp

The Holy Quran is the miraculous word of Allah Subhan-hu Wa-Ta’ala, given as a source of guidance for all humanity until the end of time. In today's world, where everything is measured against scientific discovery, the Quranic revelations from 1,400 years ago continue to astonish leading scientists. While the Quran is not a textbook of science, it is a "Book of Signs," and every sign aligns perfectly with modern scientific understanding.

1. Creation of the Universe: The Big Bang and the Quran

Modern Astronomy confirms that the universe began with a massive expansion, commonly known as the "Big Bang." Scientists believe that initially, all matter and the heavens were a single entity (Singularity) before they were split apart.

Remarkably, the Quran mentioned this in Surah Al-Anbiya: "Have those who disbelieved not considered that the heavens and the earth were a joined entity, and We separated them?" (21:30). This verse points directly to the core of the Big Bang theory. Such deep truths hidden within the Success of Quranic Wisdom inspire both believers and seekers of truth.

2. Human Embryology: Seven Stages of Life

Modern human embryology only advanced in the 20th century. However, the Quran described the stages of human development in the womb with incredible precision in Surah Al-Mu'minun: from a drop of fluid (Nutfah) to a clinging clot (Alaqah), then a chewed-like lump (Mudghah), followed by the formation of bones and their covering with flesh.

Professor Keith Moore, a world-renowned authority on embryology from the University of Toronto, stated: "It is impossible for Muhammad (PBUH) to have known these minute details 1,400 years ago; it must be a revelation from God." Understanding the Importance of Early Quran Learning allows us to recognize these divine signs early in life.

3. The Boundary Between Two Seas: Oceanography

Oceanography has discovered that where two different seas meet, there is an invisible barrier that maintains their respective temperatures, salt levels, and densities. They don't mix immediately.

Allah Almighty revealed in Surah Ar-Rahman: "He released the two seas, meeting [side by side]; Between them is a barrier [so] neither of them transgresses." (55:19-20). This is a physical reality that scientists now observe through advanced diving missions and satellite imagery.

4. The Origin of Iron: A Gift from Space

Science tells us that iron was not originally formed on Earth. Instead, it was brought here by meteorites from outer space. The Quran specifically uses the word "sent down" for iron. In Surah Al-Hadid, Allah says: "And We sent down iron, wherein is great military might and benefits for the people." (57:25). This precise terminology is a testament to the Quran's authenticity. Memorizing the Quran preserves these profound realizations for generations.

5. Mountains as Pegs for Stability

Modern Geology confirms that mountains have deep roots underground that help stabilize the Earth's crust (plates). The Quran describes mountains as "Awtada" (Pegs). In Surah An-Naba, Allah states: "Have We not made the earth as a wide expanse, and the mountains as pegs?" (78:6-7). This function of providing stability is critical for the habitability of our planet.
