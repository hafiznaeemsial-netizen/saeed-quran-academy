Why Learning Tajweed is Essential

Tajweed means "to make better" or "to perfect." In Quranic studies, it refers to the rules governing the pronunciation of the letters when reciting the Quran.

Preserving the Meaning

In Arabic, a slight change in the pronunciation of a letter can completely change the meaning of a word. Reciting without Tajweed can lead to errors that change the message of Allah's word. Therefore, learning Tajweed is a duty for every Muslim.

The Beauty of Recitation

Reciting with Tajweed brings out the natural melody and beauty of the Quranic verses. It allows the reciter and the listener to feel the depth and emotion of the revelation.
